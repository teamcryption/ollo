# Brand icons: trademark notice

The `brands` category (`brand-*` icons) shows logos of other companies and projects so people can link to or mention those platforms. Every logo is a trademark of its owner. The Library does not own these marks and does not imply endorsement.

- **Free to use, not part of any paid tier.** Brand icons are offered free; they are never sold as Library artwork.
- **Use them to refer to the brand only**, e.g. a "Sign in with GitHub" button or a link to an Instagram profile. Follow each owner's brand guidelines (linked in each icon's `meta.json` under `brand.guidelines` where known). Do not alter the marks to imply a different product.
- **Source.** Shapes come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.38.0 (CC0 1.0 collection), scaled to the Library's 24px grid (live area 3–21). The original brand colour is recorded in `brand.hex`.
- **Not included** either because they are not in the Simple Icons set (most were removed there at their owners' request) or because their licence or brand policy rules out a commercial library: LinkedIn, Slack, Adobe and Photoshop/Illustrator, Canva, Microsoft (Windows, Edge, Teams, VS Code, Skype), Amazon, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Salesforce, Disney+, Prime Video, Nintendo Switch, InVision, Affinity, Heroku, Vue.js and Sass (non-commercial licence), webpack and Fedora (restrictive brand policy), and the Coinbase, WooCommerce and American Express wordmarks (unreadable at icon size).
